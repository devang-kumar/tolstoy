Frequently asked questions

What is Tolstoy AI Player?
+

AI Player is Tolstoy's shoppable video player for eCommerce websites. It displays your videos in feeds, stories, and carousels across your site, and lets customers add products to cart directly from the video. AI Player handles the entire video experience: importing content, tagging products, optimizing performance, and tracking conversions.

What can I do with AI Player?
+

AI Player gives you everything you need to turn video into a sales channel.

Core capabilities:

  • Shoppable video feeds on PDPs, homepage, collection pages, and landing pages
  • Auto-import from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Google Drive
  • AI-powered tagging that matches videos to products and variants automatically
  • Dynamic media gallery that personalizes PDP content based on visitor behavior
  • A/B testing that identifies top performers and serves them automatically
  • Syndication to Walmart homepage and Shop App at no extra cost‍
  • Email and SMS integration through Klaviyo flows or optimized GIFs for other providers

Learn more about shoppable video solutions.

What results can I expect from AI Player?
+

Brands using AI Player see significant improvements in engagement and revenue.

Typical results:

  • Up to 307% conversion uplift on pages with shoppable video
  • Up to 4x increase in time on site from engaged viewers
  • Up to 3x AOV uplift through improved product discovery

Skin Rocks saw a 14% conversion rate lift after adding UGC and video to their PDPs.

What is the Tolstoy Syndication Network?
+

Tolstoy is the only platform that syndicates shoppable videos to Walmart and Shop App for free. Your videos appear on high-traffic spots like the Walmart homepage carousel and the Shop App home feed, putting your content in front of millions of shoppers at no extra cost. Even free Tolstoy users can submit videos to Walmart syndication.

Learn more about Shop App videos.

What platforms does AI Player integrate with?
+

AI Player integrates with all major eCommerce platforms and tools.

Supported platforms:

  • eCommerce: Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, VTEX, Wix
  • Mobile apps: Tapcart, Shop App
  • Email and marketing: Klaviyo, Yotpo
  • Support: Gorgias

Shopify users get one-click installation with automatic catalog sync. Other platforms require two code snippets and a product catalog CSV.

Learn more about Tolstoy's integrations.

How does the dynamic media gallery work?
+

The dynamic media gallery replaces your default PDP gallery with a personalized, interactive experience. It automatically shows different images and videos based on each visitor's behavior, location, or traffic source. Shoppers can also choose a model that matches their size or style. Every interaction is tracked, and AI Player A/B tests creatives automatically to serve top performers.

Learn how to create product videos that convert.

Will AI Player slow down my site?
+

No. AI Player uses lazy loading, deferred video delivery, and a sub-50KB JavaScript payload to ensure zero impact on Core Web Vitals. Videos only load when customers scroll to them, not on initial page load. The multi-CDN architecture delivers content from the server closest to each visitor for maximum speed.
